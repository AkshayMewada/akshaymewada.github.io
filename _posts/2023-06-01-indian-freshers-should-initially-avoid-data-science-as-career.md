> *Data Science has become a buzzword in the job market, and it is considered one of the most sought-after career options.*

*With the rise of Big Data, many young professionals are drawn toward data science as a career choice.   
However, Indian freshers should initially avoid Data Science as a career for several reasons.*

- Skillset for Data Science
- Challenging Job Market
- Understanding Business Context

**Skillset for Data Science**

- *Data Science requires a specific skill set that may not be available to Indian freshers.*
- *It demands expertise in mathematics, statistics, computer science, and programming languages like Python and R.*
- *While some Indian graduates may possess these skills, many do not have the required knowledge to pursue a career in Data Science.*

**Challenging Job Market**

- *The job market for Data Science in India is competitive, and the demand for experienced professionals is high.*
- *With so many experienced professionals in the market, it may be challenging for Indian freshers to find entry-level positions in Data Science.*
- *Companies usually prefer experienced candidates who can deliver results from the start.*

**Education and Exposure**

- *The Indian education system may not provide the necessary exposure to Data Science.*
- *The curriculum of most Indian colleges is outdated and does not provide hands-on experience with the tools and technologies used in the industry.*
- *This lack of exposure can be a significant disadvantage for an Indian fresher who wants to pursue a career in Data Science.*

**Understanding Business Context**

- *Indian freshers may face difficulty in understanding the business context of data science.*
- *Data Science is not only about crunching numbers and writing code but also about understanding the business problem and providing a solution.*
- *Indian freshers may lack the experience and skills to translate business problems into data science solutions.*

***While Data Science is an exciting career option, Indian freshers should initially avoid it.***

- *Instead, they should focus on building a strong foundation in mathematics, statistics, and computer science.*
- *They should also gain practical experience by working on projects, participating in hackathons, and attending workshops.*
- *Once they have gained the necessary skills and experience, they can consider pursuing a career in Data Science.*

*By doing so, Indian fresher will increase their chances of success in the Data Science job market.*
